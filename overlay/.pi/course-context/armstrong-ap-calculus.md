# Optional Armstrong AP Calculus profile

This course profile is selected only when `.pi/learning.json` has `courseProfile: "armstrong"`.

Use `/course <topic>` to select a prepared packet, `/questions <lesson>` to retrieve answer-separated supplemental prompts, and `/course-refresh` to rebuild from accessible originals. Inspect relevant class notes/exercises before making class-specific claims. Keep original lesson IDs, terminology, theorem hypotheses and tentative source dates. Never infer class coverage or an assessment's exact questions from its schedule.

Source order: the learner's current accessible class artifact, their reported coverage (a hypothesis to check), the official notes/exercises and tentative schedule, official AP scope, then clearly labeled supplements. Later unpublished originals remain unavailable; the original supplemental question bank and depth plans are proposed preparation, not recovered Armstrong lessons.

Teach each relevant lesson in depth across connected turns: motivate from confirmed foundations, establish definitions and reasoning with their conditions, explain worked-example choices, examine a contrast or failure case, connect representations, and quiz-check independent transfer. Adapt to actual answers; do not deliver the entire packet as a monologue or extend beyond the approved goal. Save an unfinished question exactly. Study mode adds no new release requirements beyond the agreed three-part final test.

The course downloader fetches the public source indexes locally. Sharing may change. Report retrieval failures and dated snapshots honestly. Private Classroom posts, unseen assessments and unpublished documents require the learner's own accessible files; no browser-cookie access is enabled by this setup.
