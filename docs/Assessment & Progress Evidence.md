# Assessment & Progress Evidence

[[Course/Runtime Teaching Contract]] · [[Course/Required Lesson Guide Fields]]

Each actual reviewed attempt records its prompt, purpose, difficulty, representation, strand/node, quiz toolCallId, correct/incorrect selection, reasoning judgment, observed reasoning and error categories. Sound reasoning must come from the submitted quiz Note or a real subsequent learner message. F4 dictation is raw learner evidence: confirm ambiguous signs, words and variable names before judging. Do not rewrite a ramble into a correct explanation.

- **Concept:** wrong definition, hypothesis, dependency or mental model.
- **Algebra:** a calculation/manipulation slip despite a sound conceptual setup.
- **Notation:** ambiguity or misuse of variables, signs, derivative symbols or expressions.
- **Graph:** incorrect interpretation of slopes, coordinates, intervals or graphical behavior.
- **Context:** units, quantities, domain restrictions or interpretation inconsistent with the problem.
- **Unclassified:** insufficient evidence; probe before deciding. Multiple categories may coexist.

These are reviewed observations, not diagnoses inferred from one selected distractor. A wrong answer triggers a surrounding clarification before a prerequisite conclusion. A correct choice with unsound reasoning stays unverified.

**VERIFIED** means a sound direct check followed by a different fresh changed-representation transfer check on the same approved node, both with actual reasoning and tutor reviews. The pair must remain on the active session branch. A later unsound attempt invalidates the current pair until new direct and transfer evidence follow repair. **LEGACY_CHECKED** preserves an already-confirmed old map without pretending it met the new pair standard. **UNVERIFIED** means the current runtime lacks that evidence; it does not erase prior historical checkpoint claims.

New checkpoint understanding requires both quiz IDs with the same exact claim text in `evidence`. A new confirmed node records its transfer ID. Checkpoints retain the runtime snapshot, observed errors, original evidence and exact next step. Cancellation/pause preserves the prompt and never records the correct answer or an imagined attempt. Archived checkpoints retain earlier observations.

`/test-prep` selects matching saved checkpoint weaknesses and their error observations. Older weaknesses without categories remain usable. It does not treat absence of evidence as failure, manufacture coverage, or predict private tests. Delayed review starts with retrieval before summaries. Lock release remains governed by the existing three-part `study_test` validator.
